# Walkthrough - AI Job Search & Application Agent

We have built a state-of-the-art, self-contained AI Job Search & Application Agent in the `jobapplier` directory. The application consists of a **FastAPI backend** that doubles as a static file server to deliver a **custom, premium dark-mode dashboard** without needing Node.js/NPM.

---

## 🌟 Key Features Implemented

1. **Dashboard & UI Interface**:
   - High-fidelity dark mode styling, card glow, and clean fonts.
   - Live timeline activity stream reporting searches, tailoring, and lifecycle changes.
   - Status indicators verifying Gemini API key connectivity.
2. **AI Resume & Cover Letter Tailoring (`tailor.py`)**:
   - Integrates with Gemini (`gemini-2.5-flash`) to parse base resumes and job descriptions.
   - Generates tailored resumes in Markdown and crafts customized cover letters.
   - Converts Markdown resumes and cover letters into clean, print-ready PDF files using Playwright.
3. **Guided Manual Application Workflow**:
   - Presents an application checklist alongside editable tailored materials.
   - Persists and downloads a tailored resume PDF and cover-letter TXT with safe, readable filenames.
   - Opens the verified employer posting while leaving all form answers and submission decisions under the user's control.
4. **Job Posting Search & Match Crawler (`searcher.py`)**:
   - DuckDuckGo crawling to find Lever & Greenhouse links matching target keywords.
   - Automated profile matching score (0-100%) and key bulleted match analyses.
5. **Applications Database & Logging (`database.py`, `utils.py`)**:
   - Tracks found jobs and matches.
   - Automatically queries Gemini to find the U.S. Headquarters location of companies.
   - Logs: Company name, Position title, Date applied, U.S. Headquarters location, Status, and downloadable resume and cover-letter files.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Usage Guide

1. **Configure Your Profile**:
   - Click the **Profile & Resume** tab in the dashboard.
   - Enter your contact details, personal links (GitHub, LinkedIn, Portfolio), and paste your **Gemini API Key**.
   - Copy-paste your base resume text or upload a `.txt`/`.md` resume file.
   - Click **Save Settings**.
2. **Search for Jobs**:
   - Go to the **Search & Match** tab.
   - Enter job keywords (e.g., `"React Developer Remote"`) and location.
   - Click **Search & Analyze**.
   - JobApplier will query Yahoo Search for supported ATS postings and display valid matches with a score (e.g. `85%`) and key pros/cons.
3. **Tailor Materials**:
   - Click **Tailor Materials** next to a matched job.
   - The AI will generate a tailored PDF resume and cover letter.
   - Click **View Materials** to review the tailored cover letter text and check the output path of the resume.
4. **Complete the Application**:
   - Click **Apply Manually**, review and save any edits, then download the **Resume PDF** and **Cover Letter TXT**.
   - Click **Open Application Site** and complete the employer's form yourself.
   - Return to JobApplier and click **Mark Applied** to record the actual date, method, notes, and follow-up date.
5. **View Logs**:
   - Inspect the **Application Logs** tab to view Company, Position, Application Date, U.S. Headquarters, and status, and download both generated artifacts directly.
